SMS / text message program

When you call Barbarossa Barbershop and book, reschedule, or cancel an appointment, you verbally provide your mobile number and agree to receive a text message confirming your appointment details and a verification code used to cancel or reschedule. This is the only way we message you — there is no list, no marketing, and no bulk texting.

  • What you receive: a one-time confirmation text per booking, reschedule, or cancellation you request.
  • Message frequency: only when you take an action on the phone; typically 1–3 texts per visit.
  • Message & data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.

Privacy policy

Barbarossa Barbershop collects your name and mobile phone number only to book and manage your appointment and to send you the appointment confirmation described above. We do not sell your information.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers are used solely to deliver the appointment-related text messages you requested. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.
